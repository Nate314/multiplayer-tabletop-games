# Chinese Checkers

Hop your ten marbles across the star into the opposite point.

Players are seated in join order with these colors: Player 1 red, Player 2 blue, Player 3 green, Player 4 yellow, Player 5 purple, Player 6 orange. Each player starts in one point of the star, taken clockwise in seat order, so turns also pass clockwise around the board:

- 2 players: Player 1 at the bottom, Player 2 at the top
- 3 players: the bottom, the upper left and the upper right points
- 4 players: the bottom, lower left, top and upper right points, two opposite pairs
- 6 players: every point, starting at the bottom

Each player sees their own point at the bottom of the board.

## Rules

- The board is a six-pointed star of 121 holes. Each player has ten marbles, which start filling their point, and races them into the point opposite
- Two, three, four or six players can play. Five cannot, because one player would have no opponent sitting opposite: with five seated, the game does not start
- On your turn, move one of your marbles. Either step it to an adjacent empty hole, or hop it over one adjacent marble (yours or anyone's) into the empty hole straight beyond
- A hop can be followed by more hops with the same marble, in any direction, and you may stop after any of them. A chain of hops may not land on the same hole twice or end where it began. A step cannot be combined with hops
- Marbles are never captured: a hopped marble stays where it is
- The first player whose opposite point is full, with at least nine of the ten holes holding their own marbles, wins and the game ends. One opponent marble left in your point counts toward filling it. A move can complete another player's point, and then that player wins. Everyone else is ranked by how many of their own marbles are in their opposite point
- A player with no legal move passes, with a message. If nobody can move, the game ends and the players with the most marbles home win
- A player who disconnects keeps their seat and marbles, and can rejoin. If the others dismiss them, their marbles are removed from the board and play goes on. The last player left wins
- Any player can ask to undo the last move, a whole chain of hops at once. It is undone only if every player still in the game approves

## Controls

- On your turn, the marbles you can move glow. Click one, then click a marked hole to move there. A step is sent at once
- After a hop, the holes the marble can hop to next are marked. Click the next one to keep going, or click End move beside Undo to stop there. When no further hop is possible, the move is sent by itself
- Click the picked marble again to start over, or click another of your marbles to choose that one instead
- A line follows the path built so far, and a faint line shows the last move. The board is dimmed when it is not your turn
- Click Undo to ask everyone to take back the last move
- Click Rules at the top of the page for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide to the rules and controls
- With Color-blind symbols on in Settings, each player color also shows its shape: red circle, blue square, green triangle, yellow star, purple cross, orange hexagon. Marbles and the player list show it

## Differences from Wikipedia

- Each player plays one set of ten marbles. Wikipedia lets two or three players control two or three sets, and two players play with 15
- The game ends when the first player fills their target. Wikipedia plays on for second and later places. Everyone else is ranked by marbles home
- One marble left in your target cannot stop you: you win once every hole there is occupied and at least nine of the marbles are yours. Wikipedia has no such rule
- A chain of hops may not land on the same hole twice. Wikipedia does not say
- A player with no legal move passes. Wikipedia does not say
- The fast-paced and capture variants are not implemented
