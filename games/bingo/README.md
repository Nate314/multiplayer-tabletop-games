# Bingo

Take turns calling numbers, mark your cards and shout Bingo first, for 2 to 12 players.

## Rules

- **Cards.** Each card has five columns under B I N G O: B holds five numbers from 1 to 15, I from 16 to 30, N from 31 to 45, G from 46 to 60 and O from 61 to 75. The center square is free and counts as marked. Every player gets the same number of cards
- **Calling.** Players take turns calling. On your turn, Call draws the next of the 75 numbers, in a shuffled order nobody can see, and the turn passes on. Seat 1 calls first, and joining closes after the first call
- **Marking.** Any player can mark a square on their own cards at any time. Only a called number can be marked
- **Claiming.** Any player can shout Bingo! at any time. The claim is checked against the player's marks on all their cards, so a called number that is not marked does not count. A claim without the pattern is refused with "Not a Bingo yet", with no penalty
- **Places.** A valid claim wins the next place at once. That place stays open until the next Call or three seconds after its first claim, whichever comes first, and everyone who claims while it is open shares it. A placed player keeps their calling turn, but their cards are done: no more marks or claims
- **End.** The game ends when the last place the house rules play until is filled, when every player has a place, or ten seconds after the 75th call. Players without a place rank after every place, all tied
- **House rules.** Cards per player (1 to 12, standard 1). Pattern: Line (any full row, column or either diagonal, standard), Four corners or Blackout (every square). Play until 1st place (standard), 2nd place or 3rd place
- Cards are not secret, so a shared device can play for several players
- Any player can ask to undo the last call or Bingo. It is undone only if every connected player approves. Marks are never undo steps. An undone call puts the uncalled numbers in a new order, so undo never shows what comes next
- **Leaving.** The game goes on while two or more players remain. A leaver's cards go with them, and a placed player who leaves keeps their place. If everyone left has a place, the game ends

## Controls

- When it is your turn, click Call
- Click a square on your card to mark it. A called number you have not marked yet is softly highlighted on every card that holds it
- Click Bingo! to claim. It checks all your cards at once
- Tick Auto-call to have a number called for you one second after your turn comes up. Tick Auto-mark to have every called number marked on your cards, and Bingo shouted for you as soon as a card has the pattern. Both are kept only in your own browser tab and are off again when the page reloads
- On a shared device every player on it has their own labelled section of cards and their own Bingo! button, and Auto-call and Auto-mark work for all of them
- The top shows the last call large, every call so far, and the pattern being played. Cards sit two across on a phone and four across on a wide screen, and the page scrolls
- A table screen and spectators see the called numbers board, 1 to 75 in five rows with the called ones lit
- Click Undo to ask everyone to undo the last call or Bingo
- Open the ☰ menu and choose History to step through every call and claim, and open the ☰ menu and choose Rules for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide to the rules and controls

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bingo_(American_version)) for 75-ball cards. Where the article leaves a choice open, or this game changes it:

- **Turn-based calling.** In a hall one caller draws the balls. Here the players take turns calling, one number per turn
- **The claim window.** Online, two players can press Bingo! a moment apart. Here a place stays open until the next Call or three seconds after its first claim, and everyone who claims in that time shares it
- **Auto-call and Auto-mark** are not part of the game; they only call and mark for the players in your own browser tab
- **The final window.** If nobody claims after the 75th call, the game ends ten seconds later, with nobody placed who has not claimed
