# Yahtzee

Roll five dice up to three times a turn and fill every scoring box on your sheet, for 2 to 8 players (up to 10 with the house rules override). The highest total after 13 rounds wins.

## Rules

- **Turns.** Players take turns in seat order. A turn is up to three rolls: roll all five dice, then hold any dice and roll the rest again, up to twice more. You may score after any roll
- **Scoring.** After rolling, you must write the dice in one empty box on your sheet. There are 13 boxes, so the game lasts 13 rounds. A box that does not fit your dice may be filled with a zero, and a box is filled only once
- **Upper section.** Ones to sixes score the sum of the dice showing that number. If the upper section totals 63 or more, a bonus of 35 is added
- **Lower section.** Three of a kind and four of a kind score the sum of all five dice. Full house (three of one number and two of another) scores 25, small straight (four in a row) 30, large straight (five in a row) 40, Yahtzee (five of a kind) 50, and chance the sum of all five dice
- **Yahtzee bonus.** Each Yahtzee rolled after 50 points are in the Yahtzee box earns 100 points. If the Yahtzee box holds a zero there is no bonus
- **Joker rule.** A Yahtzee rolled when the Yahtzee box is already filled (with 50 or a zero) must go in the upper box for its number if that is empty. If it is full, it must go in any empty lower box, where full house, small straight and large straight score their full 25, 30 and 40. If the whole lower section is full, it scores a zero in an empty upper box
- **Winning.** When every player has filled all 13 boxes, the highest total (boxes, upper bonus and Yahtzee bonuses) wins. Players with equal totals share the win
- **Leaving.** A player who leaves keeps their seat and can rejoin. Dismissing them skips their turns from then on, and their sheet is not counted. If only one player is left, that player wins
- Any player can ask to undo the last roll or score. It is undone only if everyone else approves. A roll that is undone and made again may come out differently

## Controls

- Click Roll to roll all five dice. The dice and the rolls left are shown to everyone
- Rolled dice tumble while held dice stay still; the score slides from the dice into its box
- Click a die to hold it (it gets a green outline), then click Roll again to roll the rest. Roll again is off once you hold all five dice or have no rolls left
- After your first roll, each empty box in your column shows the points the dice would score there. Click one to write it in. Under the Joker rule only the boxes you may use can be clicked
- The score sheet has a column per player, with the player on the move marked, an upper section subtotal and bonus, the Yahtzee bonus and each player's total
- Click Undo to ask the other players to undo the last move
- Click Rules at the top of the page for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide to the rules and controls

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow the standard and official Yahtzee rules in [Wikipedia's Yahtzee article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahtzee), with the forced Joker rule. What is different:

- **Players.** The game needs 2 to 8 players, or up to 10 when the host ticks the override, and everyone must agree to the table before play starts. There is no solo game
- **Joker rule.** The article describes both the official forced Joker and a free choice variant. Only the forced Joker is played
- **Holding every die.** A roll that holds all five dice would change nothing, so it is refused. Score instead
- **Held dice.** The dice you hold are sent with the roll and shown to everyone as outlined. Dice held in the previous roll start held again, and you can release them
- **Leaving.** The article does not cover a player leaving mid-game. Here a dismissed player is skipped and not counted in the final result. If only one player is left, they win
