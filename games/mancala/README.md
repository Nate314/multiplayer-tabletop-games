# Mancala

Move stones around the board and collect the most in your bank.

Two players. The first to join moves first.

## Rules

- The board has six pits per player and a bank at each end. Every pit starts with four stones
- On your turn, pick up all the stones in one of your non-empty pits and drop one stone in each following hole, counter-clockwise. Your own bank gets a stone; your opponent's bank is skipped
- If your last stone lands in your own bank, you take another turn
- If your last stone lands in an empty pit on your side and the pit across has stones, you capture both: that stone and every stone across go into your bank
- As soon as either row is empty, each player moves the stones left on their side into their own bank and the game ends
- The player with the most stones in their bank wins. Equal banks are a draw
- If a player leaves mid-game, the other player wins
- Any player can ask to undo the last turn. It is undone only if the other player approves

## Controls

- Your row is always along the bottom, with your bank on the right; your opponent's row and bank are at the top and on the left
- Arrows around the board show the counter-clockwise direction the stones travel
- Each bank is tinted in its owner's color and labeled with their name, or Your bank for your own
- The first player's stones are blue and the second player's are red. Stones keep their color as they move, so a bank shows whose stones it captured
- Each pit and bank shows its stones and a count. A pit draws up to 18 stones one by one and a bank all 48; the count is always exact. The bank totals are also listed under the board
- On your turn, click one of your outlined pits to play it
- A move animates: the stones are picked up, dropped one at a time along the way, then captured stones fly to the bank, and at the end of the game the rows are swept into the banks. Nothing animates when your system asks for reduced motion
- The pit the last move started from has a dashed outline and the hole where its last stone landed has a solid one
- The board is dimmed when it is not your turn
- The status shows extra turns, captures and the final score
- Click Undo to ask the other player to undo the last turn
- Click FAQ at the top of the page for the rules and controls

## Differences from Wikipedia

None. This is Kalah with six pits and four stones per pit, the standard rules Wikipedia describes, without its optional variants (empty capture, other end-of-game counting). Wikipedia calls the stones seeds and the banks stores.
