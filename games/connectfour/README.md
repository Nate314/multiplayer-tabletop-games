# Connect Four

Drop discs into the grid and line up four in a row.

Exactly two players: Player 1 plays Red and moves first, Player 2 plays Yellow.

## Rules

- The grid has 7 columns and 6 rows, and starts empty
- Players take turns dropping one of their discs into a column that is not full. The disc falls to the lowest empty cell in that column
- The first player to line up four of their discs in a row, horizontally, vertically or diagonally, wins
- If the grid fills up with no four in a row, the game is a draw
- A player may resign at any time, on either player's turn, and the opponent wins
- Players may also agree to a draw: either player may offer one at any time, and the opponent accepts or declines. The offer lapses when they decline or make a move
- If a player is dismissed mid-game, the other player wins
- Either player can ask to undo the last disc. It is undone only if the other player approves

## Controls

- On your turn, drag your disc from the tray above the board onto a column to drop it there. Or click the arrow above a column, or click anywhere in the column, to drop a disc there. The arrow of a full column is greyed out
- The last disc dropped is outlined in yellow, and the winning four glow green
- The board is dimmed when it is not your turn
- Click Undo to ask your opponent to undo the last disc
- Click Offer draw to offer a draw; the button reads Draw offered until it is answered. When your opponent offers, Accept draw and Decline appear in the side panel
- Click Resign and then Confirm resign to resign, at any time; click Keep playing to change your mind
- With a table screen connected, on Hand only (the default) the board shows only on your turn
- Click Rules at the top of the page for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide to the rules and controls
- With Color-blind symbols on in Settings, each disc color also shows its shape: Red circle, Yellow star. Discs and the player list show it

## Differences from Wikipedia

Rules follow [Wikipedia's Connect Four article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connect_Four). What is different:

- **Who starts.** The article says players decide who goes first. Here Player 1, who plays Red, always moves first
- **Draw offers and resigning.** The article does not cover them. Here either player may resign, or offer a draw that the other accepts, at any time
- **Leaving.** The article does not cover a player leaving mid-game. Here a dismissed player loses and the other player wins
