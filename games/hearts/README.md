# Hearts

Pass three cards, then dodge the hearts and the queen of spades in tricks, or take them all and shoot the moon, for exactly 4 players. This is the Black Lady game, which Wikipedia calls the standard game of the Hearts group in the United States, where it is usually just called Hearts.

## Rules

- **Players.** Exactly four, each playing for themselves
- **Deck.** The standard 52 cards, all dealt, 13 to each player. Ace is high and two is low; there is no trump
- **Passing.** Before play each player picks three cards and passes them: to the left (the next player) in the first hand, to the right in the second, across in the third, and no pass in the fourth, then around again. Everyone passes at once, and the cards passed to you arrive only once all four have passed. The first pass locks the seats: from then on a player who drops keeps their seat and their pass
- **First trick.** Whoever holds the 2 of clubs leads it. On the first trick nobody may play a heart or the queen of spades unless they have nothing else
- **Tricks.** Follow the suit led if you can; otherwise play any card. The highest card of the suit led takes the trick, and its winner leads the next
- **Hearts broken.** A heart cannot be led until a heart has been played, unless the leader's hand is all hearts
- **Scoring.** Each heart taken is 1 point and the queen of spades 13, 26 in a hand. A player who takes all 26 shoots the moon: they score nothing and every other player takes 26. A scoring screen shows the hand, and the next hand is dealt once every connected player has pressed Continue (disconnected players are not waited for)
- **Winning.** The game ends after the hand in which someone reaches 100 or more. The lowest score wins; equal lowest scores share the win
- You see only your own hand, the cards you passed until the pass completes, and how many cards everyone else holds. Who has passed, the points each player has taken this hand and the scores are shown to everyone
- **Leaving.** Once the first pass has been made, a disconnected player keeps their seat and the game waits for them, on their turn or for their pass (before that, a disconnect frees the seat and the cards are redealt). The game needs four, so a dismissed player ends it: the hand in progress is not scored and the lowest score among the players left wins (ties share)
- Any player can ask to undo the last card played. It is undone only if every connected player approves, and only while all four seats are filled. Passing and Continue are not moves, so an undo goes back past them

## Controls

- To pass, click three cards in your hand to pick them (click again to put one back), then click Pass 3/3. The cards you passed are shown below your hand until everyone has passed
- In a trick, the cards you may play are raised and outlined; click one to play it
- The table shows each player's score and points taken this hand, the hand number, this hand's pass and whether hearts are broken, and the four seats with you at the bottom and play going to your left. Each seat shows its card count and points this hand, and while passing whether it has passed. The middle shows the trick being played, or the last trick taken with who took it
- After each hand, read the scoring screen and click Continue; after the last hand click Close, and Show Scoring opens it again
- Click Undo to ask everyone to undo the last card played
- Click FAQ at the top of the page for a short guide to the rules and controls

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow the Modern rules and the Black Lady variant in [Wikipedia's Hearts article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hearts_(card_game)). What is different:

- **The queen of spades counts 13.** The article's Modern rules count only hearts; the queen of spades is a penalty card in Black Lady, which the article calls the standard game in the United States. This game plays Black Lady
- **Passing order.** The article says the direction changes each hand but gives no fixed order. Here it is left, right, across, then no pass, repeating. Everyone passes at once, and the first pass of the game locks the seats
- **The 2 of clubs leads.** The article has the player left of the dealer lead, with the 2 of clubs lead as a minor variant. This game uses the 2 of clubs lead, so there is no dealer
- **A first trick with only penalty cards.** The article suggests a misdeal when a hand holds only penalty cards. Here a player who has nothing else may play a heart or the queen of spades on the first trick
- **Leading hearts.** The article lets a leader holding only penalty cards lead a heart before hearts are broken. Here only a hand of all hearts may; a hand of hearts and the queen of spades leads the queen
- **Shooting the moon.** The article lets the shooter either take 26 off their own score or add 26 to everyone else's. Here everyone else always takes 26
- **Game end and ties.** The article gives 100 as an example target and does not cover ties. Here the game ends at 100 and equal lowest scores share the win
- **Dealing.** The article deals one card at a time. Here the shuffled deck is split straight into four hands, which gives the same chances
- **Reneging cannot happen.** A card that breaks a rule is refused, so there is no penalty
- **Reviewing tricks.** The last trick taken stays in the middle until the next card is played, and each player's points this hand are shown
- **Leaving.** The rules do not cover a player leaving mid-game. Here the game ends and the lowest score among the players left wins
