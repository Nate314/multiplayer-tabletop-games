# Cribbage

Make fifteens, pairs and runs, peg your way to 31 and race your pegs around the board, first side to 121 (or 61 if the host chose the short game), for 2 to 4 players. This is the standard game, counted for you unless the host turns on Muggins.

## Rules

- **Players.** 2, 3 or 4. At 4 the players form two partnerships sitting across the table: seats 1 and 3 against seats 2 and 4, and partners share one score
- **Deal.** The deal passes to the left after each hand. With 2 players each gets 6 cards and lays 2 in the crib. With 3 players each gets 5 and lays 1, and one card from the deck goes to the crib too. With 4 players each gets 5 and lays 1. The crib belongs to the dealer
- **First dealer.** At the start each seat is dealt one card and the lowest rank deals (ace is low); a tie is dealt again. The status line says who dealt which card
- **Laying away.** Everyone chooses their crib cards at the same time. Once all have, the player after the dealer cuts the starter. A jack as the starter scores 2 for the dealer (his heels)
- **Pegging.** Starting after the dealer, each player plays a card and the count goes up by its value (face cards 10, aces 1). The count may not pass 31. A player who cannot play says Go. A count of 15 or exactly 31 scores 2, a pair 2, three of a kind 6, four of a kind 12, and a run of three or more cards scores one point per card, in any order. When nobody can play, the last player to play scores 1 and the count starts again at 0. The last card of the hand scores 1, unless it made 31
- **The show.** After pegging each hand is counted with the starter, starting with the player after the dealer and ending with the dealer, who then counts the crib. Each set of cards adding to 15 scores 2, each pair 2, each run of three or more cards one point per card, a flush of 4 cards in the hand 4 (5 if the starter matches), and the jack of the starter's suit 1 (his nobs). The crib scores a flush only when all five cards share a suit
- **Breakdowns.** Every count shows a breakdown, such as "Fifteen two, fifteen four, and a pair is six"
- **Without Muggins.** Each count is made for you at once. After the show a scoring screen lists every count's breakdown and the next hand is dealt once every connected player has pressed Continue (disconnected players are not waited for)
- **Winning.** The first side to reach the target score (121 points unless the host chose 61) wins at once, even in the middle of pegging, so a later count of the same hand is not scored
- **Pegging board.** The board shows each side's two pegs, the front peg at the score and the back peg at the score before it
- You see only your own hand and how many cards everyone else holds. The crib stays hidden until it is counted
- **Leaving.** A disconnected player keeps their seat and the game waits for them. In a 2-player game a dismissed player loses. With 3 players the hand in progress is abandoned, the scores are kept, and a new hand is dealt for the two left. With 4 players a dismissed player breaks their partnership, so the game ends and the other team wins
- Any player can ask to undo the last move. It is undone only if every connected player approves. Cards nobody has seen are shuffled again, so an undone cut turns up a new starter. Continue is not a move, so an undo goes back past it

### House rules

- **Game length.** 121 (the standard game) or 61
- **Muggins.** Off by default. When on, the owner of a hand or the crib enters their own count and the others may take the points they missed. Pegging is still counted for you. An overclaim is corrected to the true total with no penalty. If nobody presses, the owner keeps only what they claimed. At 3 players the first opponent to press takes the points; at 4 the points go to the presser's partnership. Opponents have 20 seconds to answer Muggins! or No Muggins
- **Muggins style.** Offered only when Muggins is on:
  - **Name the points** (the standard). Opponents see only the cards and the claim. Pressing Muggins! lets the presser tap the cards of one missed combination (a fifteen, pair, run, flush or his nobs) and take its points. A wrong selection is refused with no cost
  - **Single press.** One press takes the whole difference between the claim and the true count. A press when nothing was missed costs the presser 2 points
  - **Shown total.** The breakdown is shown to everyone during the 20 seconds, and the first opponent to press takes the missed points
- Everyone must agree to the house rules before play starts

## Controls

- Click the cards you want to lay in the crib (2 with 2 players, otherwise 1), then click Send to crib. Click a chosen card again to put it back
- If you are the player after the dealer, click Cut to turn up the starter
- In pegging, the cards you may play are raised and outlined and the rest are greyed out; click one to play it. When none fits under 31, click Go
- The table shows each side's score and pegs, the seats with the dealer and anyone who said Go, the starter, the crib's card count, the cards played since the count last reset and the running total ("Count: 23")
- During the show the cards being counted and the starter are shown. With Muggins on, the owner types a whole number from 0 to 29 in Your count and clicks Claim; an opponent clicks Muggins! or No Muggins, and the time left is shown
- In Name the points, after clicking Muggins!, tap the cards of a missed combination and click Claim these (Cancel goes back)
- After each hand, read the scoring screen and click Continue; the last hand's scoring is on the game-over screen
- Click Undo to ask everyone to undo the last move
- With a table screen connected, on Hand only (the default) the seats and the starter are hidden
- Open the ☰ menu and choose Rules for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide to the rules and controls

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow the standard game in [Wikipedia's Cribbage article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cribbage). What is different:

- **First dealer.** Here each seat is dealt one card, the lowest rank deals (ace low), and a tie is dealt again automatically
- **Everyone lays away at once.** Everyone chooses their crib cards at the same time, so nobody waits
- **The cut.** The player after the dealer cuts by clicking Cut; the starter is the card at a random cut of the deck
- **Game length.** The host chooses either before play starts (everyone must agree)
- **The end of the game.** A side wins the moment it reaches the target, during pegging, his heels or the show. The show goes in order (the seat after the dealer, then the dealer, then the crib), so an earlier count can win before a later one
- **Counting.** The game counts pegging for you, and by default the show too. Muggins is the traditional self-counting game, as a house rule
- **Muggins window.** Opponents have 20 seconds to answer, and the window closes early once every opponent has answered
- **Several opponents.** At 3 players the first opponent to press takes the missed points. At 4 they go to the presser's partnership
- **Nobody presses.** The owner keeps only what they claimed, so missed points are lost even with no Muggins press
- **Overclaims.** An overclaim is corrected to the true total with no penalty
- **Name the points.** Each opponent names one missed combination per count
- **Single press.** Each opponent who presses on a correct count pays 2, but never below 0 points
- **Pegging stays automatic with Muggins.** Only the hand and crib counts are claimed. There are no pegging claims
- **Claims.** A claim is a whole number from 0 to 29
- **Leaving.** The article does not cover a player leaving mid-game. With 2 players the leaver loses, with 3 players the hand is dealt again for the two left (scores kept), and with 4 players the partnership is broken so the other team wins
