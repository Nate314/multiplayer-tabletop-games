# Risk

Raise armies, roll for battles and conquer the world, for 3 to 6 players.

## Rules

- **The map.** The classic world of 42 territories in six continents, with the bonus for holding a whole continent: North America 5 (9 territories), South America 2 (4), Europe 5 (7), Africa 3 (6), Asia 7 (12) and Australia 2 (4). Territories that touch, or are joined by a sea lane, border each other: 83 borders in all, among them Alaska and Kamchatka across the edge of the map, Brazil and North Africa, Greenland and Iceland, East Africa and the Middle East, and Siam and Indonesia
- **Cards.** 42 territory cards, each showing a territory and a symbol (infantry, cavalry or artillery, 14 of each), and 2 wild cards, shuffled into one deck. When it runs out the traded cards are shuffled into a new deck
- **Setup.** Each player has 35 armies with 3 players, 30 with 4, 25 with 5 and 20 with 6. A player is drawn at random to go first. The territories are dealt out at random, one at a time from the first player around the table, with one army on each, so the first players may get one more territory than the rest. Then, in turn order, each player places all their remaining starting armies on their own territories in one move. The first player then takes the first turn, and turns go up through the seats
- **Reinforce.** A turn starts with new armies: the number of territories you hold divided by 3, rounded down, at least 3, plus the bonus of every continent you hold whole, plus any card sets you trade. Place them on your own territories, in one go or a few at a time
- **Card sets.** Three cards of one symbol, one of each symbol, or any two cards with a wild. Trade sets while placing your armies. The sets traded in the whole game are worth 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 15 armies, then 5 more for each later set (20, 25 and so on). If a traded card shows a territory you hold, 2 more armies go onto that territory, at most once a turn. Holding 5 or more cards at the start of your turn, you must trade before placing
- **Attack.** Then attack as often as you like, from a territory of yours with at least 2 armies into a bordering enemy territory. The attacker rolls 1, 2 or 3 dice, at most one fewer than the armies in the attacking territory, since one must stay behind. The defender rolls 2 dice, or 1 when defending with a single army. The highest dice of each side are paired, then the second highest; for each pair the lower die loses one army, and a tie goes to the defender. Blitz attacks with the most dice again and again until the territory falls or the attacker has 1 army left; every roll is still made by the server
- **Capture.** When the last defending army falls, move into the captured territory at least as many armies as the dice you rolled, leaving at least one behind
- **Fortify.** Then you may make one move: any number of armies from one of your territories to another connected to it through your own territories, leaving at least one behind. That ends the turn; so does End turn without moving
- **Earning cards.** A turn in which you captured at least one territory ends with you drawing one card
- **Elimination.** A player who loses their last territory is out, and the player who took it takes all their cards. If that leaves 6 or more, they trade sets at once until they hold 4 or fewer, place those armies, and attack on
- **Winning.** Control all 42 territories. A player who is the only one left holding any territory also wins
- Hidden: each player's cards (the others see how many) and the order of the deck. A traded set is shown to everyone. The History dialog and the table view of a saved game show no cards; a saved game viewed as a player shows that player's cards
- A dismissed player's territories turn neutral (grey), keeping their armies: neutral armies never attack or move and count for nobody, but they defend with the usual dice and can be captured. Their cards are discarded. If it was their turn, the next player's turn begins; during the setup, the next player places. If they were moving into a captured territory, the dice they rolled move in first. Play goes on with two players left, and ends when only one player still holds territory
- Any player can ask to undo the last move. It is undone only if every player still in the game approves. An attack played again rolls new dice

## Controls

- The map shows each territory as a disc in its owner's color with its army count, every border as a line and every sea lane as a dashed line. Alaska and Kamchatka's lane runs off the left and right edges of the map. On a narrow window the map scrolls sideways in its own box
- With a table screen connected, on Hand only (the default) the battle, the event log and the other players are hidden, and the map shows only on your turn
- The side panel shows what you can do now. While placing armies, click your territories: each click adds 1, 5 or all that are left, as chosen with the buttons, and -1 takes one back. Click Place (or Place all in the setup). You can also drag from the Your armies tray at the top of the side panel onto one of your territories: a drop adds the same number a click would, and Place still sends it. Tapping the tray piece does nothing more, as your territories are already lit
- Attacking, click one of your territories (outlined ones can attack), then a highlighted enemy territory next to it. Choose 1 to 3 dice and click Roll, or Blitz. The source and target stay chosen between rolls. The last battle's dice are shown below the panel for everyone
- After a capture, choose how many armies move in with the slider and click Move
- Click Stop attacking and fortify, then a territory of yours and a highlighted one connected to it, choose the armies with the slider and click Move to end the turn, or click End turn
- Your cards are listed with their symbol and territory. While placing armies, pick three and click Trade
- The player list shows everyone's territories, armies, cards, whole continents and the armies they would get next turn, plus the value of the next set and the cards left in the deck. Recent events lists what happened, newest first
- Click Undo to ask everyone to undo the last move
- Click Rules at the top of the page for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide to the rules and controls
- With Color-blind symbols on in Settings, each player color also shows its shape: red circle, blue square, green triangle, yellow star, purple cross, black diamond. Territory discs (beside the army count) and the player list show it; neutral grey territories show none

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow the rules described on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_(game)) and, where it is silent, the official Hasbro rules. What remains different:

- **Dealing the territories.** The rules have players claim territories one at a time, placing one army each turn, before placing the rest one at a time. Here the territories are dealt at random with one army each, and each player places all their remaining starting armies in a single move, which is much faster online
- **Who goes first** is drawn at random instead of by rolling a die
- **The defender always rolls the most dice allowed** (2, or 1 with a single army). The rules let the defender choose to roll one die; here the attacker never waits for a defender who is away
- **Blitz** is a convenience the board game does not have: repeated attacks with the most dice, each roll still random
- **Card symbols.** Each territory card's symbol here is assigned in turn around the map (14 of each); the printed decks pair territories with symbols differently, which only matters for which territory a set's 2 bonus armies go to
- **The 2 bonus armies** for a traded card's territory go to the first such card in the set, chosen for you, at most once a turn
- **Trading** is done while placing your armies at the start of your turn, or at once after taking an eliminated player's cards when that leaves 6 or more; not at other times
- **Fortify** follows the current official rules: armies may move through any chain of your own territories. Older rules allowed only a move to a bordering territory
- **Two players, secret missions and the other variants** are not offered
- **Winning** also comes to the only player left holding territory, which matters only once neutral territories are on the map
- **Leaving.** The rules do not cover a player leaving. Here a dismissed player's territories turn neutral and their cards are discarded, and play goes on with as few as 2 players
