# Go Fish

Ask for ranks, go fish, and collect the most books of four, for 2 to 6 players.

## Rules

- A standard 52-card deck. With 2 or 3 players everyone is dealt 7 cards, with 4 to 6 players 5 cards. The rest is the stock, face down
- A random player goes first, and play goes up the seats
- On your turn, ask one other player who still has cards for a rank you hold at least one card of
- If they have any cards of that rank, they hand you all of them and you ask again
- If not, they say "go fish" and you draw the top card of the stock. If it is the rank you asked for, you show it and ask again; otherwise the turn passes to the next player. If the stock is empty you draw nothing and the turn passes
- **Books.** As soon as a player holds all four cards of a rank, they lay them down as a book, including books made by the deal
- **Running out.** A player whose hand empties draws one card from the stock at once. If the stock is empty they are out of cards: their turns are skipped and nobody may ask them
- **Winning.** The game ends when all 13 books are down. The player with the most books wins; players tied for the most share the win
- Every ask and its answer is public, listed on the table, as everyone at a real table hears it. You see only your own hand and how many cards everyone else holds. A card drawn from the stock is shown to the player who drew it, and to everyone when it was the rank they asked for. The stock is hidden from everyone
- A dismissed player's cards are shuffled into the stock and anyone out of cards draws from it. Their books stay down and still count toward the 13. If it was their turn, the next player with cards takes it. The last player left wins
- Any player can ask to undo the last ask. It is undone only if every player still in the game approves

## Controls

- On your turn, click a card in your hand to pick its rank (every card of that rank is raised), click a player on the table to ask, then click Ask. With only one other player holding cards, they are picked for you
- The table shows every player in seat order with their card count and books; the player whose turn it is is outlined. Beside it are the stock and the ask log, newest first
- A card you just drew is shown in the status panel
- With Highlight newly drawn cards on in Settings (the default), the card you fish from the stock is outlined until your next ask; cards handed to you are not
- Click Undo to ask everyone to undo the last ask
- With a table screen connected, on Hand only (the default) the other players are hidden except while you pick whom to ask, and the middle of the table is hidden
- Click Rules at the top of the page for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide to the rules and controls

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow the rules described on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Go_Fish). Wikipedia gives several endings and variations; this game uses these:

- **The game runs until all 13 books are down**, one of the endings Wikipedia gives and the one in the [Bicycle rules](https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/go-fish), rather than ending when the first player runs out of cards
- **Drawing the asked rank goes again.** Wikipedia's base rules pass the turn after any go fish and list going again as a variation; here drawing the rank you asked for lets you ask again. Completing a book with any other drawn card does not
- **After a go fish the turn passes to the left** (the next seat up), as in the Bicycle rules, not to the player who was asked
- **Who goes first** is not given by Wikipedia; here a random player starts
- **An empty hand draws one card at once.** The Bicycle rules have that player draw on their own turn; here they draw straight away, so the current player always has someone to ask
- **Six players.** Wikipedia suggests two to five (some sources say six); the deal for 4 or more players is used for six too
- **Leaving.** The rules do not cover a player leaving mid-game. Here their cards are shuffled into the stock and their books stay down
