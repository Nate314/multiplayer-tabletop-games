# Steady Hand

Steer each part out of its twisting slot in a robot repair bay without touching the walls, highest score when the bay is empty wins, for 1 to 8 players.

## Rules

- **The bay.** Ten parts sit in narrow, twisting slots: the Bolt (8 points), Fuse (5), Servo (12), Gear (15), Spring (20), Valve (22), Lens (26), Chip (32), Sensor (35) and Coil (40). Short, wide slots are easy and score little; long, narrow ones score more
- **Turns.** Players take turns in seat order, seat 1 first. On your turn, pick any part still in its slot and drag it along the slot to the round exit at the far end
- **Out.** A part that reaches the exit is out and scores its value for you, and the turn passes
- **Buzz.** If the part touches a slot wall, the bay buzzes, your turn ends and the part goes back to its rest spot for anyone to try. Dragging off the edge of the bay counts as touching a wall
- **Letting go.** Letting go before the exit puts the part back with no buzz, and it is still your turn
- **Buzzes allowed** (house rule). Each player may buzz 3 times (the standard at every table size), or 1 or 5, or without limit. A player who has used all their buzzes is out of turns and is skipped
- **Wall tolerance** (house rule). Normal is the standard; Easy widens every slot and Hard narrows it
- **End.** The game ends when every part is out, or when nobody has buzzes left. The highest score wins; equal top scores share the win
- **Solo.** Playing alone is a score attack: your score is the value of the parts you got out, plus 10 points for each buzz you have left if you cleared the whole bay (no bonus under unlimited buzzes)
- **Live play.** Everyone else watches the held part move as it is dragged. Only finished turns (a part out or a buzz) are steps in History; the dragging is not
- **Undo.** Undo takes back the last turn when everyone agrees

## Controls

- On your turn, press a part with the mouse or a finger and drag it to the round exit at the end of its slot. Each part's value is shown under it
- The scores list shows every player's points and buzzes left, with the player to move highlighted
- Parts already out are listed under "Out of the bay" with their value and who brought them out
- Click Undo to ask the table to take back the last turn; everyone else votes on it
- History shows the bay after each turn, with the part moved highlighted and the spot of a buzz marked
- Click Rules at the top of the page for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow [Wikipedia's Operation (game) article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_(game)). What is different:

- **The bay.** An on-screen repair bay with original parts and slots stands in for a patient and tweezers; the pointer or a finger is the tool
- **No cards or money.** Wikipedia's game deals cards that name which piece a player must remove and pays money for it; here you pick any part still in a slot and it scores its points
- **Buzzes.** A buzz ends your turn and the part stays in its slot for anyone to try. The Buzzes allowed house rule can put players out of turns
- **Solo and tolerance.** The solo score attack with its bonus for unused buzzes, and the Wall tolerance house rule, are not in Wikipedia
- **Trust.** Each player's own browser checks whether the part touched a wall, and the game trusts it
