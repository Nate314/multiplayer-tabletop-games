# War

Flip cards, the higher card takes both, and win the whole deck, for 2 players.

## Rules

- The standard 52-card deck is shuffled and dealt out evenly, so each player has a face-down pile of 26. Nobody sees the order of any pile, their own included
- **Battle.** Each player turns up the top card of their pile, and the higher card takes both. Aces are high and suits do not matter
- **Flipping online.** Each player turns up their own card, and each flip is a move. The player whose turn it is flips, the turn passes to the other player, and the battle is settled when the second card is turned up. The player who flipped second starts the next battle. Seat 1 flips first
- **War.** On a tie, each player puts one card face down and one card face up, and the higher face-up card takes every card in the battle. Another tie is another war. One flip puts in both of that player's war cards. As with a battle, the player who flipped second in the tied round starts the war
- **Running out in a war.** A player with one card left puts it face up, with no face-down card. A player with no cards left keeps their last face-up card for the rest of the war. If both players are out of cards on a tie, each takes back their own cards and play goes on
- Won cards go under the winner's pile in random order
- A player with no cards left loses, and the player with all 52 cards wins
- Face-down war cards stay hidden from both players, in the History dialog and in a saved game file too. Both players' card counts and the face-up battle cards are shown
- A dismissed player loses, and the other player wins
- Either player can ask to undo the last flip. It is undone only if both players approve

## Controls

- When it is your turn, click Flip, or click your own pile at the bottom of the table
- Tick Auto-flip to have your card flipped for you one second after your turn comes up. It is kept only in your own browser tab and is off again when the page reloads
- The table shows each player's pile and card count, and the cards each put into the current battle in order: war cards face down, then face up. After a battle the cards stay on the table, with the winner's side highlighted, until the next flip. The player whose turn it is is outlined
- Click Undo to ask the other player to undo the last flip
- With a table screen connected, on Hand only (the default) the other players, their battle cards and the battle summary are hidden
- Click History to step through every flip, and Rules at the top of the page for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide to the rules and controls

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_(card_game)) for two players. Where the article leaves a choice open:

- **Flipping in turn.** At a table both players turn up their cards at the same time. Here each player flips their own card as a move, one after the other, and the battle settles on the second flip
- **Running out of cards during a war.** Wikipedia lists several ways to handle this. Here the player uses their last card face up, and that card stands for the rest of the war. Wikipedia does not say what happens if both players run out on a tie; here each takes their own cards back
- **Order of won cards.** Wikipedia notes that the rules do not fix the order. Here won cards go under the winner's pile in random order, since a fixed order can make a game loop forever
- **Two players only.** Wikipedia also describes three and four player games; this game is for two
- **Auto-flip** is not part of the game; it only flips your own card for you
- **Leaving.** The rules do not cover a player leaving. Here a dismissed player loses
