# Trading Floor

Trade cards blind with everyone at once until you hold all 9 of one commodity, then press Corner!, first to the target score (500 unless the host chose 250), for 3 to 8 players.

## Rules

- **No turns.** Everyone trades at the same time, whenever they like
- **Commodities.** Saffron is worth 100, Silver 85, Coffee 80, Cocoa 75, Copper 70, Sugar 60, Cotton 55 and Soy 50. A table of n players plays the first n of that list: three players play Saffron, Silver and Coffee
- **Deal.** The deck holds 9 cards of each commodity in play, shuffled, and each player is dealt 9
- **Offers.** Pick 1 to 4 cards of one commodity and offer them. Everyone else sees only how many cards you offer ("Alice: 3!"), never which. You have at most one open offer: a new one replaces it. You can withdraw it at any time. The first offer of the game starts play
- **Swaps.** Anyone with an open offer of the same number of cards can accept another player's offer, and the two offers swap blind: each player gets the other's cards
- **Already taken.** If two players accept the same offer at once, the first to reach the server swaps. The other sees "Already taken" for a moment and loses nothing: their cards and their own offer stay as they were. An offer that was withdrawn or replaced before an accept arrives is also already taken
- **Corner.** Whoever holds all 9 cards of one commodity may press Corner! and wins the round, scoring that commodity's value. The round ends at once and every open offer is dropped
- **Rounds.** After a corner everyone sees the round scoring: the cornered hand and every player's total. When everyone still connected presses Continue, new hands are dealt and the next round starts
- **Winning.** When a corner brings anyone to the target score (500, or 250 by house rule), the game ends and the highest total wins; equal totals share the win
- **Bull and Bear** (house rule, off by default). One Bull and one Bear join the deck, so two players are dealt 10 cards. Either may ride along in any offer with cards of one commodity, or be offered alone, within the 4-card limit. The Bull is wild for a corner: 8 of a commodity plus the Bull corners for that commodity's value, and all 9 plus the Bull corners for double. A player holding the Bear cannot corner. When a round ends, whoever holds the Bear loses 20 points, and so does whoever holds the Bull without having cornered with it
- **Leaving.** A player who disconnects has their open offer withdrawn. If a player leaves for good mid-round, the round is called off and dealt again for those left. Below three players the game ends, and the scores as they stand decide it
- **No undo.** There is no Undo
- Only swaps and corners are steps in History; offers and withdrawals are not

## Controls

- Your hand is grouped by commodity. Click cards to pick them (click again to put one back), then click Offer. Your offered cards are shown raised
- Click Withdraw to take your offer back, or pick other cards and click Offer again to replace it
- The trading floor lists every player with their card count and score, and their open offer as a count. Beside another player's offer, Accept is enabled when your own open offer has the same number of cards
- Corner! is enabled only when your hand makes a corner
- After a corner, read the round scoring and click Continue; the last round's scoring is on the game-over screen
- History shows each swap and corner, with who moved highlighted, and no player's hand
- A spectator sees the floor, the card counts and the offers' counts, but no hand
- Open the ☰ menu and choose Rules for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow [Wikipedia's Pit (game) article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pit_(game)). What is different:

- **Commodities.** The commodities and their values (Saffron 100 down to Soy 50) are original, in place of the article's grains. The commodities in play are the first ones in that list, one per player
- **Target score.** Wikipedia gives no target score. Here the game plays to 500, or 250 by house rule
- **No bell.** There is no bell to ring: the Corner! button ends the round, and trading starts as soon as anyone makes an offer
- **Bull and Bear alone.** Wikipedia lets the Bull and the Bear be traded alone or with cards of one commodity; here the same (an offer may be only the Bull or the Bear), within the 4-card limit
- **Bear and corners.** Wikipedia says the Bear's holder cannot declare a corner; here the same
- **Typed offers.** Offers are blind but typed, not shouted: each offer shows its count beside the player's name, and each player has one open offer at a time
- **No undo.** There are no turns, and taking back a swap would undo every offer made since, so there is no Undo
- **Leaving.** A player leaving mid-round calls the round off and deals again for those left; below three players the game ends with the scores as they stand
