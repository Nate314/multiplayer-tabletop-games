# Shape Race

Fit every shape into its hole before the board pops, for 1 to 8 players.

## Rules

- **The race.** Everyone gets the same holes and the same shapes, each on a board of their own, and all race at once against one shared countdown. There are no turns
- **Board size** (house rule). 9, 16 or 25 shapes (16 is the standard). Shapes start turned at random, so most need a few taps before they fit
- **Timer** (house rule). 30, 60 or 90 seconds (60 is the standard). The clock starts for everyone when the last player agrees to the house rules, and the server keeps the time, so a slow phone or connection changes nothing. A placement that reaches the server at or after the end does not count
- **Race end** (house rule, offered with two or more players). Keep racing (the standard): the first to fill their board wins, the others keep racing until they finish or the board pops, and the race ends early once everyone has finished. Places go by finish time, then by shapes placed. First finish: the race ends the moment anyone fills their board, and everyone else is ranked by shapes placed
- **Placing.** Drag a shape onto its hole. It drops in only for the shape the hole was cut for, turned so it looks the same as the hole. A wrong drop, or a release off the board, springs the shape back and costs only the time it took. A placed shape and a filled hole stay put
- **Pop.** When the time runs out the boards spring up and the unplaced shapes scatter. If nobody placed anything, the status reads "The board popped! Nobody placed a shape", and that pop is shared by everyone
- **Winning.** The earliest finish wins. If nobody filled their board, the most shapes placed wins and equal counts share the win
- **Ties.** Finishes the server receives in the same 50 ms tick share the place; the server's arrival order decides, never a time sent by a browser
- **Solo.** Playing alone, you win by filling the board before the pop, and your time is shown
- **Watching.** During the race you see only your own board, and for each other player a bar with their count, such as "Ana 12/16", with a check mark once they have finished. Your board and what you placed show to everyone after the race. Spectators see every board side by side. The table screen shows the bars
- **Pause.** A pause keeps the race's remaining time: the clock stops, and on resume the race has the time it had left
- **Leaving.** The race continues for the players left, and the leaver's board goes with them. With Keep racing, if everyone left has finished, the race ends at once with the finishers ranked
- **No undo.** There is no undo; a wrong drop already springs back
- **Rematch.** The game over dialog's Rematch button starts a new race with a new layout

## Controls

- Tap a shape in the tray to turn it one eighth of a turn (45 degrees) clockwise. A round shape fits whichever way it faces
- Press a shape with the mouse or a finger and drag it over its hole on your board, then let go to place it
- A drag the browser cancels, such as one a phone scroll gesture interrupts, springs the shape back and sends nothing
- The timer above the board counts down; the bars show how far each other player has got
- History shows the board after each placement, with the last placed hole outlined
- Open the ☰ menu and choose Rules for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide
- It works on a phone: the board and tray shrink to fit the screen

## Differences from other games

Shape Race is an original game: a timed shape-sorting race with its own shapes and art. The shared clock, the Keep racing and First finish rules and the same-tick tie are this game's own design.
