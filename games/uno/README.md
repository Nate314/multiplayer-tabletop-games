# UNO

Match colors and numbers, empty your hand to score the cards left in the others, and reach the target score first (500 unless the host chose another), for 2 to 8 players (up to 10 as a house rule).

## Rules

- The standard 108-card deck: in each of four colors one 0, two each of 1 to 9, and two each of Skip, Reverse and Draw Two, plus 4 Wild and 4 Wild Draw Four cards
- **Dealer.** For the first round each player draws a card and the highest number deals. A card that is not a number counts lowest, and tied players draw again. The deck is reshuffled, everyone is dealt 7 cards and the top card starts the discard pile. The player to the dealer's left (the next seat up) goes first. The deal passes one seat on each round
- **Starting card.** It acts as if the dealer had played it: a Skip skips the player to the dealer's left, and a Draw Two makes them draw 2 and lose their turn. After a starting Reverse the dealer plays first and play runs the other way. After a starting Wild the player to the dealer's left names the first color, then plays. A starting Wild Draw Four is shuffled back into the deck and another card is flipped
- On your turn, play a card that matches the top of the discard pile by color, or by number or symbol, or play a Wild and choose the next color
- Instead you may draw one card. If it can be played you may play it at once or pass to keep it; otherwise your turn ends
- Skip: the next player loses their turn. Reverse: play changes direction (with two players it acts as a Skip). Draw Two: the next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn
- **Wild Draw Four.** You choose the color, and it may be played only if you hold no card of the current color (cards matching only the number, and Wilds, do not count). The game lets you play it anyway, as in the physical game. The next player either accepts, drawing 4 and losing their turn, or challenges. On a challenge only the challenger sees your hand. If you held a card of that color you draw 4 instead and the challenger takes their turn; otherwise the challenger draws 6 and loses their turn
- **UNO.** Call UNO when you play your second-to-last card. A late call still counts if nobody has caught you yet. Until the next player makes their move, any other player may catch a player who has not called it, and the caught player draws 2
- **Scoring.** A round ends when a player plays their last card. If it is a Draw Two or Wild Draw Four the next player draws those cards first. The player who went out scores the cards left in everyone else's hands: number cards at face value, Skip, Reverse and Draw Two 20 each, Wild and Wild Draw Four 50 each. A scoring screen shows the round, and the next round is dealt once every connected player has pressed Continue (disconnected players are not waited for). The first player to reach the target score wins (500 unless the host chose another)
- **Stacking (house rule, off by default).** A Draw Two or Wild Draw Four does not make the next player draw at once. That player may answer with another Draw Two on a Draw Two, or a Wild Draw Four on either, which passes the growing count on to the next player, or accept: they draw the whole count and lose their turn. A Draw Two may not go on a Wild Draw Four. A Wild Draw Four can be challenged only while it is the only card in the stack. A stacked card needs no match in color. A starting Draw Two still draws at once. If a stacked card is a player's last, the next player draws the whole count before the round is scored
- **7-0 (house rule, off by default).** Playing a 7 swaps your hand with any other player's, whom you choose. Playing a 0 passes every hand one seat in the direction of play. A 7 or 0 that goes out does neither. A player left with one card by a swap or a pass has no UNO to call and cannot be caught for it
- **Jump-in (house rule, off by default).** Any other player may play a number card (0 to 9) out of turn if it is identical in color and number to the top card. Play then continues from the jumper in the current direction, so the player whose turn it was loses it and any card they had drawn. A jumped 7 or 0 still swaps or passes hands with 7-0 on, and a jump-in that empties the hand goes out. It is not allowed while a Draw stack or Wild Draw Four is waiting, during the scoring screen, or before the starting color is named. Two players jumping on the same card is a race: the first move the server receives wins and the other is refused. A jump-in is a move, so it closes the UNO window like any other and can be undone
- **Draw until you can play (house rule, off by default).** Drawing takes cards until one can be played or both piles are empty. You may play that card or pass; if nothing could be played your turn ends
- When the draw pile runs out, the discards except the top card are shuffled into a new draw pile
- You see only your own hand and how many cards everyone else holds, apart from a hand you challenge. The draw pile is hidden from everyone
- A dismissed player's cards go to the bottom of the draw pile and their turns are skipped. A Wild Draw Four played by or on them is dropped. The last player left wins
- Any player can ask to undo the last move. It is undone only if every player still in the game approves. Calling UNO, catching and Continue are not moves, so an undo goes back past them too

## Controls

- Playable cards in your hand are raised and outlined; click one to play it
- To play a Wild, click it and then pick a color, or click Cancel to put it back. After a starting Wild the color buttons appear for the first player
- Click Draw, or the draw pile, to draw a card. After drawing, play the highlighted card or click Pass to keep it
- With two cards in your hand, click UNO! before playing your next-to-last card; it stays pressed until you play. If you forgot, click UNO! right after playing. When another player has not called UNO, a Catch button appears for everyone else until the next move
- With jump-in, a card you could jump in with is raised and outlined even when it is not your turn; click it to play it
- With the 7-0 rule, click a 7 and then the player to swap hands with, or Cancel. With stacking, the table shows the cards waiting to be drawn; play a highlighted Draw Two or Wild Draw Four on it, or click Accept to draw them
- When a Wild Draw Four is played on you, click Challenge or Accept and draw 4. After a challenge, the challenged hand is shown to you below the table until your next move
- The table shows the round, every player's card count and score in seat order, the dealer, an arrow for the direction of play, the top discard and the current color. The player whose turn it is is outlined
- After each round, read the scoring screen and click Continue; the last round's scoring is on the game-over screen
- With Color-blind symbols on in Settings, each color also shows its shape: red circle, blue square, green triangle, yellow star. Colored cards, the current color and the color buttons show it; Wild cards carry no color
- With Highlight newly drawn cards on in Settings (the default), the cards you draw are outlined, including those a Draw Two or Wild Draw Four makes you take, until your next move (the 2 cards a caught missed UNO makes you draw are not outlined)
- Click Undo to ask everyone to undo the last move
- Click Rules at the top of the page for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide to the rules and controls

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow the classic official rules described on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uno_(card_game)), which play to 500 points. The host can choose 250 or 1,000 instead, and everyone must agree to that before play starts. What remains different:

- **House rules.** Stacking Draw Twos and Wild Draw Fours, Seven-O and drawing until you can play and jump-in are not part of the official rules, so they are off unless the host turns them on and everyone agrees. Other house rules Wikipedia lists are not available
- **Stacking details.** Wikipedia describes stacking only loosely. Here a Draw Two goes on a Draw Two, a Wild Draw Four goes on either, and a Draw Two never goes on a Wild Draw Four. A stacked Wild Draw Four cannot be challenged
- **Seven-O details.** The 7 may swap with any other player, and a 0 passes hands in the direction of play. Going out with either swaps nothing
- **Mattel's 2022 rule changes are not followed.** Since 2022 the official goal is just to go out first in a single hand, there is no official way to choose the dealer, and a starting action card or Wild is ignored and another card flipped until a number card appears. This game keeps the classic rules instead: play to 500, cut for the dealer, and starting cards take effect
- **Cutting for the dealer.** Wikipedia says only that the highest number deals. Here a card that is not a number counts lowest and tied players draw again
- **Calling and catching UNO.** At a table a player shouts "UNO" and anyone may catch them. Here the call is a button, and the catch window closes when any move is made, which is the next player beginning their turn
- **Leaving.** The rules do not cover a player leaving mid-game. Here their cards go under the draw pile, and a Wild Draw Four played by or on them is dropped
- **Players.** This game seats 2 to 8, or up to 10 when the host raises Most players. With 10 players the 108 cards deal 7 each and leave 38 to draw from
