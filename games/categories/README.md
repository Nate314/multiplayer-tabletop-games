# Categories

Everyone gets the same letter and the same list of categories, and races the clock to write an answer for each that starts with the letter. Answers nobody else gave score, and the highest total after the rounds wins. For 2 to 8 players. It follows Wikipedia's [Categories (game)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categories_(game)) article.

## Rules

- **Rounds.** The host sets 1 to 5 rounds (3 unless changed). Each round has one random letter and one list of categories
- **Letters.** The letter comes from A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, P, R, S, T and W. With the Hard letters house rule on, Q, U, V, X, Y and Z join them. A letter is never used twice in a game
- **Lists.** Each list has 8, 10 or 12 categories (12 unless changed), drawn from a built-in pool of at least 150. A category is not used twice in a game
- **Answering.** Everyone writes one answer per category, up to 40 characters, within the time limit: 60, 90, 120 or 180 seconds (120 unless changed). The clock starts when everyone has agreed to the house rules. Answers stay hidden from the other players until the round ends
- **Done.** A player can press Done to stop early. The round ends when the timer runs out or when everyone has pressed Done
- **Valid answers.** An answer must start with the round's letter. A leading "a", "an" or "the" is skipped when checking, so "The Tiger" is valid for T and "The Eagle" is not. A blank answer and an answer with the wrong letter score nothing
- **Scoring.** 1 point for each valid answer no one else gave. Answers that are equal once capitals, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes are ignored, and a leading "a", "an" or "the" is skipped, are duplicates and score 0 for everyone who gave them. "a dog" matches "Dog" but not "dogs"
- **Challenges.** In the review, any player can challenge another player's scoring answer. Everyone except its author votes Keep or Reject, and the challenger's own vote counts as a reject. More than half of the voters must reject for the answer to score 0.
- **Next round.** After the review, each player presses Continue. A new challenge clears every Continue, so the round cannot end while a vote is still owed
- **Winning.** After the last round the player with the highest total wins, and equal top totals share the win

## Controls

- **Answer sheet.** One box per category. Type an answer; it is sent to the server shortly after you stop typing, when you leave the box, and at once when a second or less is left, so the review shows what you saw
- **Done.** Press when you have finished, to wait for the others
- **Challenge.** Under another player's scoring answer in the review
- **Keep and Reject.** Vote on a challenge, under the answer that was challenged
- **Continue.** Press when the review is settled. "Waiting for" names the players who still have to vote or press Continue
- **House rules.** The host sets Rounds, Time per round, Categories per list and Hard letters before play starts, and everyone must agree

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow the rules in [Wikipedia's Categories (game) article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categories_(game)). What is different:

- **Choosing the categories.** The article has the players agree on a list together. Here the game draws a list of 8, 10 or 12 categories from a built-in pool
- **Choosing the letter.** The article picks a letter at random. Here a random letter comes from 20 letters, without Q, U, V, X, Y and Z unless the Hard letters house rule is on, and a letter is never repeated in a game
- **Time limit.** The article gives a set time without a length. Here it is 60 to 180 seconds, and Done ends the round early once everyone has pressed it
- **Scoring.** The article has players swap sheets and score each other's entries: 2 points for an entry no one else has and 1 point for a shared one. Here the game checks the letter and the matches by itself, and scores 1 point for a valid answer no one else gave and 0 for a duplicate
- **Matching.** The article does not say when two entries match. Here capitals, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes are ignored, and a leading "a", "an" or "the" is skipped, also when checking the letter
- **Challenges.** The article has no challenge rule. Here any player can challenge a scoring answer and the other players vote, with the challenger's own vote counting as a reject and more than half of the voters needed to reject. A challenge reopens Continue for everyone
- **Winning.** The article gives the win to the highest total. Here equal top totals share the win
- **No undo.** Answers, challenges and votes cannot be taken back
- **Leaving.** The article does not cover a player leaving. Here a player who is dismissed is dropped from the scoring: their answers, the challenges on them and their votes go, and the round carries on with the players left
