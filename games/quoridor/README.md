# Quoridor

Race your pawn to the far side and block opponents with walls.

Players are seated in join order: Player 1 (blue) starts at the bottom, Player 2 (red) at the top, Player 3 (green) on the left, Player 4 (purple) on the right.

## Rules

- Players take turns either moving their pawn or placing a wall
- Pawns can move one square orthogonally (up, down, left, right)
- Pawns can jump straight over an adjacent pawn if the square beyond is free and not walled off
- If a wall, a third pawn or the board edge is behind the adjacent pawn, the pawn may instead step diagonally to either side of it, unless a wall or pawn is in the way. Walls and two pawns in a row can never be jumped
- Walls block pawn movement but cannot completely block a player's path to their goal
- The twenty walls are shared out equally: 10 each with two players, 6 each with three (two stay unused), 5 each with four
- First player to reach their opposite edge wins
- If a player leaves mid-game, their pawn leaves the board, their walls stay, and their turns are skipped
- Any player can ask to undo the last turn. It is undone only if every player still in the game approves

## Controls

- Click "Move" and then click a valid square to move your pawn
- Click "Horizontal Wall" or "Vertical Wall" to place walls
- Or drag your pawn onto a highlighted square, with the mouse or a finger; drop it anywhere else to put it back
- Your walls sit in a tray under the action buttons, with the count left. Tap the wall to pick it up (horizontal) and a dot on the bottom right corner of a square marks each place it can go, the middle of the wall it would make; tap it again, click "Rotate" or press R to turn it. Then click a dotted square, or drag the wall from the tray onto one
- Valid moves (diagonal ones included) and wall placements are highlighted
- The game board is dimmed when it's not your turn; its frame stays colored to show each player's goal side
- Click "FAQ" at the top of the page for FAQs on moving, placing walls and the rules
- Click Undo to ask everyone to undo the last turn; it happens only if every player still in the game approves
- With Color-blind symbols on in Settings, each pawn color also shows its shape: blue square, red circle, green triangle, purple cross. Pawns and the Goals list show it

## Differences from Wikipedia

- Wikipedia says the twenty walls are divided equally but gives no count for three players. Three players get 6 walls each and the two left over are not used.
