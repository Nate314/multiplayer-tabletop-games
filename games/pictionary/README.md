# Pictionary

Draw a word while everyone else races to guess it in the chat, for 2 to 10 players. Each player draws 3 times, and the highest score wins.

## Rules

- **Turns.** Players draw in seat order. Each turn has three parts: the drawer chooses a word, draws it while everyone else guesses, and then the word is shown to all
- **Choosing.** The drawer is offered 3 words and picks one. Only the drawer sees them. From the second turn on, if 20 seconds pass without a choice, one of them is chosen at random
- **Drawing.** The drawer has 90 seconds to draw the word. Letters, numbers and spoken hints are not allowed; the picture is the only clue
- **Guessing.** Everyone else types guesses in the chat. A guess is correct when it holds the word as whole words, ignoring capitals, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes, so "is it an ice-cream?" guesses "ice cream", but "catapult" does not guess "cat". The correct guess itself is never shown; the chat says that you guessed the word
- **Blanks.** Guessers see a blank for each letter of the word, with its spaces and hyphens, so they know how long it is
- **So close!** A guess one letter away from the word (one letter added, missing or wrong) tells only the player who typed it that they are close
- **Guessed-only chat.** Once you have guessed the word, what you type during the drawing is seen only by the drawer and the others who have guessed, so nobody can give the word away. The drawer cannot chat while drawing
- **Reveal.** The turn ends when time runs out or when everyone has guessed. The word is shown to everyone for 5 seconds, along with the guessed-only chat of that turn, and the next player chooses a word
- **Scoring.** A correct guess scores 10 + 90 x the share of the drawing time left, rounded: 100 for a guess at once, 55 halfway, 10 at the buzzer. The drawer scores 25 for each player who guesses the word
- **Winning.** After every player has drawn their turns, the highest score wins. Players with equal scores share the win
- **House rules.** The host can set the time per turn (30 seconds, 1 minute, 90 seconds, 2 minutes or 3 minutes), the word choices offered (3, 4 or 5) and the draws per player (1 to 5). Everyone must agree to them before play starts
- **Leaving.** A player who leaves keeps their seat and can rejoin. Dismissing the drawer ends their turn and shows the word. Dismissing a guesser ends the turn when everyone left has guessed. If only one player is left, that player wins
- Any player can ask to undo. Undo takes back the last scored guess or turn change; drawing since then is lost, and the chat goes back to that point. It is undone only if everyone else approves

## Controls

- The drawer clicks one of the offered words to start drawing
- The drawer draws on the paper with a mouse, a finger or a pen. The toolbar under the drawing has the colors, the brush sizes (Small, Medium and Large), Eraser, which paints white, and Fill, which fills the area you click. Take back removes the last line or fill, and Clear wipes the paper
- Everyone else types guesses in the chat box and presses Enter
- Click Undo to ask the other players to undo the last move
- Click Rules at the top of the page for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide to the rules and controls

## Differences from the board game

These follow the familiar rules of the Pictionary party game, played online. What is different:

- **No teams.** Everyone plays for themselves. All other players guess at once, and faster guesses score more
- **No board.** There is no board, no category squares and no die. Every player draws the same number of times, and points decide the winner
- **Chat guessing.** Guesses are typed in the chat instead of shouted, and the game checks them, so nobody has to judge a guess
