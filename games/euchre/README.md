# Euchre

Make trump with your partner and take at least three of the five tricks, with the jacks on top, first team to the target score (10 unless the host chose another), for exactly 4 players. This is the standard North American game.

## Rules

- **Teams.** Four players in two partnerships, partners sitting across: seats 1 and 3 against seats 2 and 4
- **Deck.** 24 cards: 9, 10, jack, queen, king and ace in each suit
- **Deal.** Seat 4 deals the first hand and the deal passes to the left after each hand. Each player gets 5 cards, the next card is turned up, and the other three stay face down in the kitty
- **Making trump, first round.** Starting left of the dealer, each player may order up the turned card, making its suit trump, or pass. The dealer may pick it up the same way. When it is ordered up, the dealer adds the turned card to their hand and discards any one card face down
- **Making trump, second round.** If all four pass, the turned card is turned down. Starting left of the dealer again, each player may name any other suit as trump, or pass. The turned-down suit may not be named. If all four pass again, the hand is thrown in and the next player deals
- **Going alone.** The player who made trump may go alone. Their partner sits out the hand and the other three play tricks of three cards
- **Bowers.** The jack of trump (the right bower) is the highest card, and the other jack of the same color (the left bower) is next. The left bower counts as a trump for the whole hand, not as its own suit. Then come the ace, king, queen, 10 and 9 of trump. Other suits run ace high down to 9
- **Tricks.** The player left of the dealer leads the first trick, and the winner of each trick leads the next. Follow the suit led if you can; otherwise play any card. The highest trump takes the trick, else the highest card of the suit led
- **Scoring.** If the team that made trump takes 3 or 4 tricks it scores 1 point; all 5 (a march) scores 2, or 4 for a lone player. If it takes fewer than 3 it is euchred and the other team scores 2. A scoring screen shows the hand, and the next hand is dealt once every connected player has pressed Continue (disconnected players are not waited for)
- **Winning.** The first team to reach the target score (10 points unless the host chose another) wins
- You see only your own hand and how many cards everyone else holds. The kitty stays hidden except the turned card, and only the dealer sees the card they discarded
- **Leaving.** A disconnected player keeps their seat and the game waits for them on their turn. A dismissed player breaks their partnership, so the game ends and the other team wins
- Any player can ask to undo the last move. It is undone only if every connected player approves, and only while all four seats are filled. Continue is not a move, so an undo goes back past it

## Controls

- In the first round, click Order up (Pick up for the dealer) or Pass. In the second round, click a suit to name it trump, or click Pass
- If you made trump, click Go alone or Play with partner
- If you are the dealer and the turned card was ordered up, click a card in your hand to discard it
- In a trick, the cards you may play are raised and outlined; click one to play it. Your hand is sorted by suit with trump, the left bower included, at the right
- The table shows both teams' scores and tricks, the hand number, trump and who made it, and the four seats with you at the bottom and your partner across. Each seat shows its card count, the dealer, a pass this round, the maker and a partner who sits out. The middle shows the turned card while trump is made, then the trick being played, or the last trick taken with who took it
- The dealer sees the card they discarded below their hand
- After each hand, read the scoring screen and click Continue; the last hand's scoring is on the game-over screen
- Click Undo to ask everyone to undo the last move
- Click Rules at the top of the page for these rules, and FAQ in it for a short guide to the rules and controls

## Differences from Wikipedia

These follow the standard game in [Wikipedia's Euchre article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euchre). What is different:

- **Target score.** The article plays to 5, 7 or 10 points. This game plays to 10 unless the host chooses 5 or 15 before play starts (everyone must agree)
- **Dealing.** The article deals in batches of 2 and 3. Here the shuffled deck is split straight into four hands and the kitty, which gives the same chances. Who deals first is not in the article: seat 4 deals first
- **The dealer's discard.** The article does not say which card the dealer may discard. Here any card may go, the turned card included
- **Going alone.** The article does not say who leads when a player goes alone, or what happens to the turned card when the dealer sits out. Here the player left of the dealer leads, or the next player if that one sits out, and a dealer who sits out does not pick up the turned card
- **No lone defenders.** The article mentions a regional rule where a defender may also go alone. That is not offered here
- **Reneging cannot happen.** The article has a penalty for not following suit when able. Here a card that does not follow suit when it must is refused, so there is no penalty
- **Reviewing tricks.** The last trick taken stays in the middle until the next card is played, and the number of tricks each team has taken is shown
- **Leaving.** The rules do not cover a player leaving mid-game. Here the partnership is broken, so the game ends and the other team wins
