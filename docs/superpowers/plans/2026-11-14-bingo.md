# Bingo Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add Bingo (`bingo`, Party games, 2 to 12 players): players take turns calling numbers, mark their own 75-ball cards, and claim Bingo, with per-tab Auto-call and Auto-mark checkboxes.

**Architecture:** A new folder `games/bingo/` on the `GameDefinition` contract. Calling is the turn move (`applyMove`). Marking is an unrecorded action and claiming a recorded action (`applyAction` plus `recordsAction`, as in Categories and Code Breaker). A short claim window per place runs on the platform `deadline`/`onDeadline`/`resumeClock`, as in Categories. A shared device acts for each of its local seats by sending `SHOW_SEAT` before the action, which needs one small platform change: the game screen learns its device's local seats.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React 19, SCSS, `node:test`, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-bingo-design.md`, including "Settled after the builder check". Read it with this plan.

**Branch:** `game-bingo`, from `open-games-flag`. PR into `master` after `open-games-flag` merges (or into `open-games-flag` if it has not).

## Global Constraints

- Id `bingo`, name `Bingo`, category `'party'`, `minPlayers: 2`, `maxPlayers: 12`. Pass and play allowed (leave `passAndPlay` unset).
- Cards: B 1-15, I 16-30, N 31-45, G 46-60, O 61-75, five random distinct numbers per column, free center. Every player gets the same number of cards.
- House rules (`games/bingo/options.ts`, declared like `games/farkle/options.ts`):
  - `cards`, label `Cards per player`, choice 1 to 12, standard 1.
  - `pattern`, label `Pattern`, choice `line` (`Line`, standard), `corners` (`Four corners`), `blackout` (`Blackout`).
  - `playUntil`, label `Play until`, choice 1 (`1st place`, standard), 2 (`2nd place`), 3 (`3rd place`).
- Line means any full row, column or either diagonal; the free center counts as marked.
- Only a called number can be marked. A claim is checked against the player's marks only. A refused claim answers exactly `Not a Bingo yet`, with no penalty.
- A place stays open for claims until the next Call or `PLACE_WINDOW_MS = 3000` after its first valid claim, whichever is first. Everyone who claims while it is open shares that place.
- Placed players keep their calling turn; their cards are done (no more marks or claims).
- The game ends when the place named by `playUntil` closes, when every player has placed, or `FINAL_WINDOW_MS = 10000` after the 75th call. Unplaced players rank after every place, all tied.
- Auto-call (`AUTO_CALL_MS = 1000`, as War's `AUTO_FLIP_MS`) and Auto-mark are browser-only, per tab, off after a reload, and on a shared device apply to every local seat. Auto-mark also claims for you as soon as any card has the pattern.
- Undo: platform vote. Marks never become undo steps; calls and claims do. `onUndo` reshuffles the uncalled numbers.
- Leaving: the game continues while 2 or more players remain (the platform ends it below that).
- Files outside `games/bingo/`: `games/registry.ts`, `games/infos.ts`, `src/clientGames.ts`, `shared/svg/gameIcons.ts`, `shared/categories.test.ts` (party list and the game count), `games/secretState.test.ts` (`WITH_SECRET`), `src/clientGames.ts` `GameScreenProps` and `src/components/GameView.tsx` (Task 4 only), the root `README.md` (line 3 sentence and count, plus the games list), and `e2e/bingo.spec.ts`.
- No new npm dependency. No em-dashes in code comments, README, FAQ or commit messages. Commit messages end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- If `node` is missing, use nvm (`nvm use`), or prepend the nvm Node folder to PATH (memory note).

## Review Focus

1. Two players claim valid Bingos on the same call (one by Auto-mark, one by hand 2 seconds later): both share 1st; a third claim after the window closed, or after the next Call, is a later place (Task 2, `'claims inside the window share the place'`).
2. A claim with a card that has the pattern on the numbers called but not marked is refused with `Not a Bingo yet`; a mark on an uncalled number, another seat's card, an out-of-range card or square, or by a placed player is refused, never a crash (Task 2, `'forged and early marks are refused'`).
3. Undoing a Call that was followed by a claim restores the place as open or unawarded and reshuffles the uncalled numbers so the same next number is not guaranteed (Task 3, `'undo of a call reshuffles and takes back later places'`).
4. A server restart or an undo while a place window is open restarts the 3 second window from `now`, not from the stored past time (Task 3, `'resumeClock restarts an open window'`).
5. On a shared device with three local players, ticking Auto-mark marks and claims for all three, and a tap on one player's card marks only that player's card (Task 5, e2e `'pass and play marks for each local player'`).

### Task 2: Rules: calling, marking, claiming, places

**Files:**
- Create: `games/bingo/rules.ts`, `games/bingo/index.ts`
- Test: `games/bingo/rules.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1.
- Produces, in `rules.ts`: `createGameState(gameId: string): GameState`, `resetLobby(state, random): GameState` (deals `houseRule(state, CARDS_RULE)` cards per seat, empty marks, shuffled pool of 1..75), `applyMove(state, seat, move, random, now): Result<GameState>` (only `{ type: 'call' }`), `applyAction(state, seat, action, random, now): Result<GameState>` (`{ type: 'mark'; card: number; square: number }` and `{ type: 'bingo' }`), `recordsAction(state, seat, action): boolean` (true only for `bingo`), `deadline(state): number | null`, `onDeadline(state, now, random): GameState`, `resumeClock(state, now): GameState`, `onUndo(state, random): GameState`, `onPlayerLeft(state, seat, random): GameState`, `finalStandings(state): Standing[]`, `toPublicState(state, undoAvailable, viewer)`. Constants `PLACE_WINDOW_MS = 3000`, `FINAL_WINDOW_MS = 10000`.
- Produces, in `index.ts`: `bingo: GameDefinition<GameState, PublicGameState>` wiring the above, plus `secretState: state => seatFields(state, ['pool'])`. No `privateSeatView` (cards are not secret).

Rules the signatures do not fix:
- A Call first closes an open place (`openPlace = null`, checking the end condition), then moves `pool[0]` to `called`, then passes the turn with `nextActivePlayer`. The 75th Call sets `finalUntil = now + FINAL_WINDOW_MS`; with an empty pool a Call is refused (`No numbers left`).
- A valid claim sets `places[seat]` to `openPlace.place` if a place is open, else opens `{ place: closedPlaces + 1, closesAt: now + PLACE_WINDOW_MS }` where `closedPlaces` is the highest place already awarded.
- `deadline` is `openPlace?.closesAt ?? finalUntil`. `onDeadline` closes the open place, or at `finalUntil` ends the game.
- Ending sets `isGameOver`, `winners` to the seats with place 1 (empty if nobody placed), and a `gameStatus` naming the winners.

  - `'the dealt table follows Cards per player'`: with `cards` 12 and 3 players, each seat has 12 cards, the pool has 75 distinct numbers.
  - `'a call draws the next number and passes the turn'`: `called` gains `pool[0]`, `currentPlayer` moves on, `lastMove` is `{ type: 'call', seat: 1, number }`; a call by a seat out of turn is refused by the platform (not tested here).
  - `'forged and early marks are refused'`: an uncalled number, `card: 99`, `square: 25`, `square: 12` (free), a non-integer, a mark by a placed player, and a mark after the game is over each return `{ error }`.
  - `'a claim needs marks'`: a card whose Line is called but unmarked gives `{ error: 'Not a Bingo yet' }`; after marking it, the claim gives place 1 and `openPlace.closesAt === now + 3000`.
  - `'claims inside the window share the place'`: seats 1 and 2 claim at `now` and `now + 2000`, both place 1; `onDeadline` at `now + 3000` closes it; seat 3 claims later and gets place 2.
  - `'the next call closes the place'`: seat 1 claims, seat 2 calls, seat 3 claims with a valid card: seat 3 is place 2.
  - `'play until ends the game'`: with `playUntil` 1, the game is over once place 1 closes, `winners` are the place 1 seats; with `playUntil` 3 and 3 players it ends when the third place closes or everyone has placed.
  - `'four corners and blackout patterns are honoured'`: a marked Line is refused under `corners`; four marked corners pass.
  - `'the last call opens a final window'`: after 75 calls `deadline` is `finalUntil`, a 76th call is refused, and `onDeadline` at `finalUntil` ends the game.
  - `'recordsAction is true only for bingo'`.
  - `'finalStandings ranks unplaced players last together'`.
