# Cribbage Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add Cribbage (`cribbage`) for 2, 3 or 4 players (4 as partnerships), with automatic scoring and breakdowns, and Muggins as a house rule in three styles.

**Architecture:** A new folder `games/cribbage/` on the `GameDefinition` contract. Counting is a pure module (`scoring.ts`) used by the rules and the client. `rules.ts` runs the deal, discard, starter cut and pegging; `show.ts` runs the show and the Muggins window (a platform `deadline`). Scores are kept per side: a seat at 2 or 3 players, a team at 4.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React, `node --test`, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-cribbage-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Players 2 to 4. Deal: 2 players 6 cards, 2 to the crib; 3 players 5 cards, 1 to the crib, 1 from the deck to the crib; 4 players 5 cards, 1 to the crib, partnerships across the table (`shared/partnerships.ts`: team 0 is seats 1 and 3).
- House rules: `targetScore` 121 (default) or 61 via `targetScoreRule(121, [61], 'Game length')`; `muggins` toggle, standard off; `mugginsStyle` choice `name` (default, label "Name the points"), `single` ("Single press"), `shown` ("Shown total"), offered only when `muggins` is on.
- Muggins on: the hand's owner enters a number for hand and crib counts; pegging stays automatic. An overclaim is corrected to the true total with no penalty. Nobody presses: the owner keeps only what they claimed. At 3 players the first opponent to press takes the points; at 4 the points go to the presser's partnership. Single press when nothing was missed costs the presser 2 points. Shown total shows the breakdown during the window and the first opponent to press takes the missed points. A wrong Name-the-points selection is refused with no cost.
- Every count shows a breakdown in the form "Fifteen two, fifteen four, and a pair is six".
- Leaving a 2-player game: the leaver loses.
- Files outside `games/cribbage/`: `games/registry.ts`, `games/infos.ts`, `src/clientGames.ts`, `shared/svg/gameIcons.ts`, `shared/categories.test.ts`, `games/secretState.test.ts`, `games/privateSeatView.test.ts`, `README.md` (line 3 and the games list), `e2e/cribbage.spec.ts`. ## Decisions (Claude's calls, each listed in the README)

- First dealer: at deal time each seat is dealt one card from a shuffled deck, lowest rank deals (ace low), redeal ties automatically; shown in the status line. Deal passes left (`nextActivePlayer`).
- Discards are made by everyone at once (out-of-turn moves through `allowsOutOfTurn`, `waitingOn` lists seats still to discard).
- The starter is cut by the seat after the dealer as a move (`cut`); the card is the deck's top after a random cut. A Jack scores 2 to the dealer (his heels).
- The game ends the moment a side reaches the target, during pegging, his heels or the show. The show goes in order (seat after the dealer first, dealer, then crib), so an earlier count can win before a later one.
- Muggins window: 20 seconds (`MUGGINS_MS = 20000`). It closes early once every opponent has pressed Muggins! or No Muggins. A Single press penalty never takes a score below 0.
- Muggins presses and No Muggins are actions recorded as moves (`recordsAction` true); a mark of which cards a Name-the-points presser has selected is client-only.
- Leaving at 3 players: the hand in progress is abandoned, scores kept, and a fresh hand is dealt for the two left with the 2-player deal. At 4 players: `partnershipBroken`. A disconnect only waits (as Euchre does).
- No Muggins window in a game where Muggins is off: each count is applied at once, and the round summary lists every count's breakdown with Continue (`shared/rounds.ts`).

## Review Focus

1. A side reaching the target mid-pegging, or on the first show of the round: the game ends there and later counts are not scored (Task 3, Task 4 tests).
2. During a Muggins window in Name the points or Single press, no view (any seat, spectator, table screen, History) contains the true total or breakdown (Task 4 test).
3. Undo across a Muggins window: after an approved undo the window restarts from `now` through `resumeClock`, and hidden cards (deck, other hands, crib) are reshuffled where unseen (Task 4 test).
4. Pegging with Go chains: every player says Go, last card scores 1, count resets to 0, and play resumes with the next player who has cards; a 31 scores 2 and resets without a Go point (Task 3 test).
5. A flush in hand with 4 cards scores 4 (5 with the starter), but the crib scores a flush only with all 5 (Task 2 test).
