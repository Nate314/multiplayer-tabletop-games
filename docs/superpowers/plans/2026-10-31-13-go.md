**Goal:** Add Go (`go`) for two players on a 9x9, 13x13 or 19x19 board, with capture, no suicide, the simple ko rule, two passes to end play, agreed dead stones, resume play, area scoring with komi 7.5, and resign.

**Architecture:** A new folder `games/go/` on the existing `GameDefinition` contract, shaped like `games/chess/` and `games/quoridor/`. Pure board logic (chains, liberties, capture, suicide) lives in `board.ts`, counting in `scoring.ts`, and the turn and end-of-game state machine in `rules.ts`. Stones and passes are moves; marking dead stones, agreeing and resuming are game actions, and only the agreement that finishes the count is recorded (`recordsAction`), so an undo vote can take back a counted end. Resign goes through `shared/drawOffer.ts` as in Chess, with draw offers refused.

**Tech Stack:** React 19, TypeScript, SCSS, inline SVG board, `node:test`, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-nine-new-games-design.md` (sections "Rules for every game agent" and "Go").

**Branch:** `game-go` in its own worktree, from `nine-new-games`. Site port `PORT=3113`, e2e `E2E_PORT_BASE=6430` (never 6000).

## Global Constraints

- Players exactly 2. Seat 1 plays Black and moves first; seat 2 plays White. Category `strategy` ("Strategy board games").
- House rule `boardSize`, label "Board size", choices `9` "9x9", `13` "13x13", `19` "19x19", standard `19`.
- Rules (exact): capture before the suicide check; suicide forbidden; simple ko (no move may recreate the board position as it was just before the opponent's last move); positional superko not enforced; two passes in a row end play; area scoring (stones on the board plus empty points surrounded only by that color), komi `7.5` to White; dead stones marked by both players, each change clears every agreement, the count happens when both agree; Resume play continues with the player who would have moved; no handicap stones.
- Resign through `applyEndingAction` from `shared/drawOffer.ts`; `offerDraw` and `answerDraw` are refused with "Go has no draw offers".
- A player who leaves mid-game loses: the platform's `removePlayer` already ends the game for the one left, as in Chess. No `onPlayerLeft`.
- Files outside `games/go/` (one line or entry each): `games/registry.ts`, `games/infos.ts`, `src/clientGames.ts`, `shared/svg/gameIcons.ts` (the landing icon, required by `src/games/loadClientGame.test.ts`), `shared/categories.test.ts` (add `'go'` to `strategy` and the count 33 to 34), the root `README.md` game list and count, `docs/architecture.md` only where it lists games by name, `e2e/go.spec.ts`. Nothing else in `src/`, `server/`, `shared/` or `e2e/fixtures.ts`; if more is needed, stop and report.
- No new npm dependency. No em-dashes in code comments, the README, the FAQ or commit messages.
- `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint` and `e2e/go.spec.ts` pass.

## Review Focus

1. A move with bad coordinates (negative, non-integer, a string, or row 12 on a 9x9 board) must be refused with "Invalid move" and change nothing (Task 3 test `'bad coordinates are refused'`).
2. Undoing a ko capture by vote must restore the ko state exactly, so the retake is refused or allowed as before the undone move (`previousKey` is part of the snapshot) (Task 4 manager test `'an undone ko capture keeps the ko rule as it was'`).
3. After Resume play, a single pass must not end the game again, stones marked dead must be back on the board as live, and the turn is the player who would have moved (Task 4 test `'resume play clears marks and passes and keeps the turn'`).
4. An empty board, or one with stones of only one color, is counted without crashing: empty board Black 0, White 7.5; only black stones gives Black every empty point (Task 2 tests).
5. On a phone (390 px wide) a 19x19 board must place the stone on the point under the finger, not a neighbor (Task 6 e2e `'tapping a point at phone width places the stone there'`).
