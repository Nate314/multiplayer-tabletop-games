# Jenga Turntable, Placing and Camera Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Jenga gets a turntable the server simulates, drag-to-place with snapping, a Free placement house rule, turn-bound camera rules, a collapse that plays out to rest before the game-over screen, and a History that starts from the untouched tower.

**Architecture:** The turntable is a kinematic, velocity-driven Rapier body under the tower. Its rotation, spin and drive are public game state (`turntable`); the drive is set by a new `turn` action and the spin follows it each physics step with a capped acceleration, so a hard or jerky turn can shake the tower through friction. Every rule that reads block positions (footprint, slots, topple check, placement poses) works in the turntable's frame, converted with quaternion products only (no trig, so determinism holds). Free placement is a recorded `drop` move that adds the block to the world and lets the ticks decide: rests on top (turn ends), slides off (back in hand) or topples. The camera rules, the drag-to-place gesture and the turntable drawing are browser-only.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React 19, three.js, Rapier (`@dimforge/rapier3d-deterministic-compat`), node:test, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-pause-resume-jenga-and-fixes-design.md`, section 8. Index plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-31-pause-resume-jenga-and-fixes.md`. Branch `jenga-turntable` from `pause-resume-jenga-and-fixes`, in its own worktree. Server `PORT=3208`, `E2E_PORT_BASE=6550`.

## Review Focus

1. An undo or dismiss vote opening mid-turn snaps or saves the camera, or turns the camera lock off: the camera follows turn ownership, which votes do not change (Task 6, `ownsTurn` test).
2. A pass-and-play device holding two seats: each seat gets back its own saved view, not the other seat's (Task 6, camera test).
3. A room saved before the turntable (no `turntable`, no `turnStartTurntable`) loads, a turn is played on it and undone (Task 3).
4. Bad turn input: `NaN`, `Infinity`, a string, 1000 rad/s, a turn from the seat not playing, during a vote (letting go stays allowed), while the last placement settles, or while a dropped block settles: refused or clamped, never moving the tower (Task 3).
5. The turning player disconnects with the turntable driven, or is dismissed while a dropped block settles: a disconnect lets go of the drive and the settle finishes with its usual outcome; a dismissal puts the turn back, turntable angle included (Tasks 3 and 5).

**Interfaces:**
- Produces, in `types.ts`:
  - `type Turntable = { q: Quat; w: number; drive: number | null }` (comment: `w` is the spin in rad/s about +Y; `drive` is the speed the turning player asks for, null once they let go and it coasts)

- Produces, in `physics.ts`:
  - Constants `TURNTABLE`, `TURNTABLE_FRICTION`, `MAX_SPIN`, `SPIN_ACCEL`, `COAST_DECEL`, `IDENTITY: Quat = [0, 0, 0, 1]`, and `REST_TURNTABLE: Turntable = { q: IDENTITY, w: 0, drive: null }`.
  - `TowerWorld = { world; bodies; turntable: RAPIER.RigidBody }`.
  - `buildWorld(blocks: Block[], grip: (id: number) => number, turntable: Turntable = REST_TURNTABLE): TowerWorld`: the ground box drops so its top is at `y = -TURNTABLE.height`; the turntable is `kinematicVelocityBased`, a cylinder of `TURNTABLE.radius` whose top is at `y = 0`, created before the blocks, with rotation `q` and angular velocity `[0, w, 0]`. The starting tower's poses do not change.
  - `spinStep(w: number, drive: number | null): number`: moves `w` toward `clamp(drive, -MAX_SPIN, MAX_SPIN)` by at most `SPIN_ACCEL * STEP_S`, or toward 0 by at most `COAST_DECEL * STEP_S` when `drive` is null; lands exactly on the target when within one step.
  - `stepWorld(tower, grab, steps, drive: number | null = null)`: before each step, `spinStep` the turntable's current `angvel().y` and set it.
  - `readTurntable(tower: TowerWorld, drive: number | null): Turntable`.
  - `syncWorld(tower, blocks, turntable: Turntable = REST_TURNTABLE)`: also moves the turntable body to `q` and `w`.
  - `toTable(pose: Pose, table: Quat): Pose` and `fromTable(pose: Pose, table: Quat): Pose`: world to turntable frame and back (`p' = rotate(conj(t), p)`, `q' = conj(t) * q`).
  - `isOut(block, table: Quat = IDENTITY)`, `onGroundOutside(block, table: Quat = IDENTITY)`: the footprint test on `toTable(block.pose, table)`.
  - `toppled(start, now, exempt, startTable: Quat = IDENTITY, nowTable: Quat = IDENTITY)`: compares each block in its own frame (start poses in `startTable`, current in `nowTable`).
